# ShopSmart Policies

## Membership Benefits

### Basic Membership
Basic membership is free and never expires. Basic members pay a delivery fee of Rs 49 on every order and can return items within 7 days of delivery.

### Prime Membership
Prime membership costs Rs 499 for 30 days. Prime members get free standard delivery on all orders with no minimum order value. Prime members can return items within 15 days of delivery and get access to early sale events.

### Premium Membership
Premium membership costs Rs 1,299 for 30 days. Premium members get free express delivery, 30-day returns, priority customer support and early access to sales.

## Delivery

### Delivery Times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available to Premium members. Orders are processed within 24 hours of being placed.

### Order Cancellation
An order can be cancelled by the customer until it has been shipped. After shipping, the order must be returned using the returns process. Cancelled orders are restocked automatically.

## Returns and Refunds

### Return Window
Items can be returned if they are unused and in original packaging. The return window is 7 days for Basic members, 15 days for Prime members and 30 days for Premium members.

### Refunds
Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received. Delivery fees are not refunded except for defective or wrongly delivered items.

### Non-returnable Items
Gift cards and items marked as final sale cannot be returned.
